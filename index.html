Welcome to Rehab Champ 👋

We’re sorry you need this app. We know it’s a long, slow recovery, and faithful rehab makes all the difference. You’ve got one shot at getting this right, so Rehab Champ is here to cheer you on.

How it works

  • Today shows what’s due. Tap an exercise to tick it off; tap a load chip to remember your weights.
  • Each week, choose which days you’re doing gym or pool — they only appear on those days.
  • Some sessions stay hidden until your physio clears them. Enter the date on the Program tab.
  • Everything stays on this phone, so use Settings → Back up now and then.

About

Rehab Champ was designed by Dr Kate Taylor when she injured her Achilles. She is a digital health Founder, so this app has been designed based on clinical evidence and best practice — but you tailor the program based on your physiotherapist’s advice.

The app is also built with privacy by design. All your data stays on your phone — nothing is sent anywhere.
